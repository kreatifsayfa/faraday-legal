Requirements

iPhone 15 Pro or later with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. The on-device language model is what drafts your note, and older devices do not have it.

Which languages work

Apple's on-device speech model covers 30 languages. Choose yours before you record, in Settings → Session language. If you speak a language it does not cover, the transcript comes back as confident nonsense — so Faraday checks how well it understood the recording and refuses to write a note rather than inventing one.

The note came back thin or empty

That is deliberate. Faraday discards any statement it cannot trace back to the recording, and writes "Not documented this session" rather than filling a section with something plausible. Open What Faraday heard to see the transcript — if the words are wrong, the language setting or the microphone position is usually why. A phone on the desk between you works better than one in a pocket.

It got the speakers wrong

Faraday guesses who was speaking from the sound of the voices, and it cannot tell when it has got this wrong — so it asks you to confirm before attributing anything. Tap any line to change who said it, or choose I can't tell and the note will be written without naming anyone.

Where are my recordings?

Deleted. The audio is removed the moment the transcript is made. The safest recording of a therapy session is one that no longer exists.

A call interrupted the session

Recording resumes when the microphone comes back, and Faraday tells you a gap exists. It cannot know what was said during the gap, so check the note against your memory of that stretch.

Subscriptions

Five notes are free. After that a subscription covers unlimited notes — there is no per-session charge, because a note costs nothing to produce on your own phone. Manage or cancel in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Restore a previous purchase in Faraday's Settings.

Before you file a note

Read it. Faraday drafts; you decide. A draft is not a clinical record until a clinician has read it and signed off.

Faraday is a documentation aid, not a medical device. It does not diagnose and does not replace your clinical judgement.
