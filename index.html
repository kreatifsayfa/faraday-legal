Faraday

Session notes that never leave the room.

Most AI scribes are an interface. Behind them, the session audio goes to a transcription company, the transcript goes to an AI company, and the note comes back through an EHR. Three or four vendors touch the hour your client spent telling you something they have told no one else.

Faraday touches none. Recording, transcription and note drafting all run on your iPhone using Apple's on-device speech and language models. There is no account, no server, and no third party that receives protected health information — which is why there is no business associate agreement for you to review.

Don't take our word for it

Turn on Airplane Mode and use Faraday exactly as you would otherwise. Everything works, because nothing was ever going anywhere.

What it won't do

Faraday drafts; you decide. It sometimes mistakes who was speaking and cannot tell when it has, so it asks you to confirm before attributing anything to your client. It leaves a section empty rather than filling it with something plausible, and it discards any statement it cannot trace back to the recording.

Faraday is a documentation aid, not a medical device.
