What the others do with your session

Most AI scribes are an interface. Behind them the session audio goes to a transcription company, the transcript goes to an AI company, and the note comes back through an EHR. Three or four vendors touch the hour your client spent telling you something they have told no one else.

Faraday touches none. Recording, transcription and drafting all run on your Mac or iPhone using Apple's on-device speech and language models. There is no third party that receives protected health information — which is why there is no business associate agreement for you to review.

Don't take our word for it

On a Mac, the absence is checkable. Faraday's sandbox holds no network entitlement at all, so it cannot open a socket — not to us, not to anyone:

codesign -d --entitlements - /Applications/Faraday.app

On iPhone there is no equivalent, because network access there cannot be declined. So do it the other way: turn on Airplane Mode and use Faraday exactly as you would otherwise. Everything works, because nothing was ever going anywhere. An app that sends your sessions to a server cannot survive that test.

What it does

  • SOAP, DAP or BIRP drafts from the session itself.
  • The recording is deleted the moment the transcript is made.
  • Risk quoted verbatim, in a section built without the language model — the part of a note that carries legal weight never depends on one.
  • Every statement traceable back to the moment it came from. Anything that cannot be traced is discarded.
  • Couples and family sessions, with each client kept distinct.
  • Video sessions on the Mac: your microphone and the call's audio are captured separately, so Faraday always knows which of you said what.
  • Unlimited notes. No per-session charge, because a note costs nothing to produce on your own machine.
  • A consent form you can hand to a client before you record.

What it won't do

Faraday drafts; you decide. In an in-person session it sometimes mistakes who was speaking and cannot tell when it has — so it asks you to confirm before attributing anything to your client. It leaves a section empty rather than filling it with something plausible.

Read every draft before it becomes a record. And obtaining your client's consent, and complying with the recording law where you practise, remain yours.
